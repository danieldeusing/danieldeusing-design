This page is its own specimen: the bar, toolbar, TOC, rail and footer around this text are the real ones.

The page's own bar: brand in the left slot, history and the path centred over this column, the connection status and ls -l in the right slot. Back is enabled, forward disabled (the end of history), the history menu is open-able.

language switcher

In the footer (a menu of links, the current one aria-current="true", each item lang + hreflang) and in the burger's accordion. Picking a language navigates, so the current one carries the accent, not a ✓.

the burger

Below 48rem the bar's centre band gives way to the burger. Its menu holds the rail's list and the .mobile-footer: two accordions (language, theme) whose chevron turns, and every row a 44px target under a coarse pointer.

sticky layers

The toolbar above the columns sticks under the bar; the TOC sticks under the toolbar at --sticky-top, which initLsNav() measures. Every anchor lands below both (scroll-padding-block-start).
