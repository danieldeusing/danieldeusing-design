Dialogs and overlays

tokens only

src/overlays.css, src/tooltip.css, runtime/dialog.js, runtime/tooltip.js and runtime/diagramzoom.js. Every dialog is a native <dialog> opened with showModal(): the page behind goes inert, Tab stays inside, Escape closes the topmost, focus comes back. The context menu is page code on positionPopup() and attachMenuKeys().

confirm and alert

A confirm has no X: its footer is the answer pair, cancel first. An alert has no X either, and Escape and the backdrop do nothing.

drawer

The same dialog against the right edge: toolbar, a flush body holding the scrollback, the status in the foot.

tooltip

Hover or focus. Escape hides a tip and nothing else. No tip while a list is open, or on a control whose own popup is open. data-tip-parts tones a segment (size, tones); data-tip says the same words and is what a screen reader hears.
